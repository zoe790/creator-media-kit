# Recording intake

A recording is a low-effort way to provide a profile and representative posts. Do not require a profile link, browser extension, paid API, or a fresh recording when the supplied material is already usable.

## Short recording instructions for the user

Show the profile header for two to three seconds, including the name, bio, and follower count. Scroll slowly, briefly pausing on each screen. Open three to six representative posts and pause on each so the title and engagement counters are visible. There is no need to narrate or play each video in full. Roughly 30 to 90 seconds is often sufficient.

Phone and desktop recordings both work. A focused phone view or an enlarged desktop page makes text easier to read. Prefer the original recording or a compressed copy that preserves readable text. Do not require an ideal capture format if the existing one works.

## Assistant workflow

1. Inspect duration, frame dimensions, and whether the host can decode the file. Use native video understanding or available local media tools to create an overview, then inspect clear frames at useful timestamps at source resolution. Sample every three to five seconds as a starting point; revisit transitions rather than treating a sparse sample as complete coverage.
2. Select stationary profile, grid, and post-detail frames. Avoid scrolling blur, overlays, and transitions. Do not guess obscured words or figures. Skip unrelated audio transcription and exhaustive frame processing.
3. Read profile followers, following, and cumulative likes/saves as separate fields. For posts, identify title, author, visible date, and the correct like/save/comment counters. A comment's own likes are not the post's likes. Do not infer a missing year or treat a file's creation date as the date of the statistics.
4. Save original key frames and actual timestamps in the private project. A layout crop may retain a complete post card or detail panel without distortion. Preserve visible source labels and numbers. Do not construct a synthetic screenshot from elements that never appeared together. Distinguish a cover from a playback frame with `image_type`.
5. Product mentions remain unverified relationships unless explicit evidence establishes a collaboration. Do not infer backend analytics. A person visible in a post may be shown as part of that work; do not automatically repurpose a playback frame as an independent portrait.
6. Produce the brief, private research notes, and recommended previews. Consolidate only meaningful requests for additional material: for example, one clearer profile screenshot instead of asking for a complete re-recording. If decoding is unavailable, explain that limitation and accept a few key screenshots.

Keep recordings, extracted frames, and account facts out of the public skill package. Use only relevant profile/post regions in deliverables, excluding unrelated tabs, bookmarks, notifications, and conversations.
